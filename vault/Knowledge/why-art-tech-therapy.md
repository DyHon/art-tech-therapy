## Summary
The name "Art-Tech Therapy" is not a catchy label but an accurate description of the system's three-legged structure: Art, Tech, and Therapy. Art opens the door to the Unconscious, Tech structures the resulting unstructured data, and Therapy is the final destination of healing and self-understanding. ## Why is our system called Art-Tech Therapy?
This term is not a name chosen merely to sound good; it accurately reflects the three-legged tripod structure (Art - Tech - Therapy) within your matrix-chain model:

```
[ ART ] (Cinema / Film)
│
▼ (Stimulating the Unconscious)
[ TECH ] (Web System / AI / pgvector)
│
▼ (Structuring & Analysis)
[ THERAPY ] (Healing / Self-Understanding)
```

- ART (Artistic cinema): Plays the role of the "Key that opens the door to the Unconscious." According to Carl Jung, art (symbols, images, stories) is the natural language of the Unconscious. Your independent/AI film series carry open-ended questions not merely to entertain, but to touch the viewer's Shadow or latent Complexes, pulling them out of the defensive state of the Conscious mind.
- TECH (Data technology): Plays the role of the "Structuring box." The Unconscious is a vast ocean of unstructured data. Technology (Next.js, Whisper ASR, PostgreSQL, pgvector) appears to capture, securely encrypt (Zero-Knowledge), and transform those chaotic dreams and thoughts into mathematical vectors that can be computed and tracked along a time series.
- THERAPY (Therapeutic treatment): Plays the role of the "Final destination." The goal is not to create a text-typing piece of software, but to help people walk the path of the Individuation Process. The combination of Art and Tech provides both the psychology professional and the client themselves with a visual "personal map of the psyche" so they can understand themselves, dialogue with the Shadow, and heal in the most scientific way possible.
